Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Humble Learners ("the app") is a members-only app for the Humble Learners community in Gombe, Nigeria. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Email address and password. You sign in with your email and a password. Your password is stored only in protected (hashed) form by our backend provider; nobody can read it. Only community admins see your email (partly hidden, for approving join requests); other members never do. We email you only for account matters, such as a password-reset code.
  • Name and laqab. Shown to other members. No profile photos are collected or shown.
  • What you post. Messages, voice notes, images and book links in discussions, visible to active members.
  • Reading data. Your reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, likes and pages read. These are private to you. Other members only see how many books you have read if you turn that on in Settings. Admins see only community totals (for example, books finished this month), never who read what.
  • Event responses. Whether you are going to an event (counts are visible to members).
  • Last active time. To show an "online" dot, only if you allow it in Settings.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or sharing of data with third parties for marketing.
  • No location tracking. Prayer times use the community's location set by admins, not your phone's location.

Where data is stored

On our backend provider (Supabase), which also stores the library books. No other service receives your data. Data on your phone (downloaded books, cached messages) stays on your phone and is removed when you sign out or remove downloads in Settings → Data & storage.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications and privacy options on or off in Settings.
  • Edit your name and laqab in Profile.
  • To delete your account and data, ask a community admin or email muhdnasrahmd@gmail.com.

Children

The app is for members of the community; it is not directed at young children.
