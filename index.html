Humble Learners

Humble Learners is a members-only mobile app for the Humble Learners community in Gombe, Nigeria. It brings the community's announcements, discussions, library, reading and events together in one place.

Members only. There is no public sign-up. Members sign in with their Google (Gmail) account and join with an invite code from the community admins, or ask to join and wait for an admin to approve.

What's in the app

  • Community home with announcements, upcoming events and prayer times
  • Topic-based discussions, questions and answers
  • A library and reader for books shared by the community (works offline)
  • Events with reminders
  • English and Hausa

No ads, no tracking, and no profile photos.
